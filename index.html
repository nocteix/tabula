TABVLA · ΠÍΝΑΞ

hundreds of words, paradigms, and practice sentences across both

Tabula holds Latin and Greek side by side — flashcards, paradigm tables, sentence parsing, and accent drills, all in one place.

Everything you get right or wrong is remembered in this browser, so you can close the tablet and pick the stylus back up later.

Scroll down and choose a mode to start learning.

Choose where to start

  • vocab · λέξεις

    Vocab start here

    Flip through flashcards that push what you know further out and pull what you don't back to the front.
  • paradigms · τύποι

    Paradigms

    Type a full declension or conjugation into the table by hand, checked cell by cell as you go — or reveal it.
  • parsing · ἀνάλυσις

    Parsing

    Click any word in a practice sentence and name its case, mood, or voice. Your answers stay marked when you return. Sample sentences only — not quotations from ancient authors.
  • accent · τόνοι

    Accent

    Spot which syllable is accented and what kind of mark it carries in Greek, or whether a marked vowel is long or short in Latin.
  • progress · πρόοδος

    Progress

    See at a glance how many words, forms, and sentences you've got down — and what still needs work.
